Please remind yourself that your worth as a person has nothing to do with numbers on a scale, calorie counts, or body shapes. Recovery is challenging, but you deserve a peaceful, healthy relationship with food and your body. If possible, consider speaking with an eating disorder specialist or a trusted health professional.
